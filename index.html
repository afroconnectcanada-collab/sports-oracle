How accurate is it? walk-forward backtest on real results

Every game from July 2024 to Nov 7, 2026 was predicted using only games played before it (ratings refit weekly), then scored against the final result. Model settings were tuned by searching for the lowest log loss. "Before tuning" is the same model with starting settings.

Brier score measures probability accuracy: 0 is perfect and always guessing the home team's average win rate scores the "Baseline" column. Lower is better. NHL games are close to a coin flip for every model; even the betting market only picks about 58–60% of NHL winners.

The model

Every team gets an offense rating (points or goals scored above an average team) and a defense rating (points allowed above average; lower is better). Ratings are opponent-adjusted: each one is solved together with every opponent's, so beating good teams counts more than beating bad ones.

  • Projected score = league average + your offense + opponent's defense ± half the home advantage (learned from the data each season).
  • Season carry-over: each season starts from last season's ratings, pulled partway back to average, then this season's games take over.
  • Tuned per sport: how much to carry over, how fast new games override the prior, whether recent games count more, how to treat blowouts, and how much a back-to-back hurts (NBA ≈ 2 points, NHL ≈ 0.45 goals).
  • Win probability: football and basketball margins follow a bell curve (width fit by backtest). Hockey uses a Poisson goal model with overtime, so it handles puck lines and totals properly.

Finding value

Enter a sportsbook line and the app compares the model's probability with the break-even probability of the price (52.4% at −110). Positive edge means the model rates the bet above what the price requires. Stakes use quarter-Kelly.

Using it well

  • Keep it current: add finished games in Add results (or paste a day's scores) so the ratings stay fresh. They're saved to your account.
  • Injuries and news aren't in the scores yet. Use the adjustment box: a star quarterback out is often worth 4–7 points, an NBA star 3–6, a starting goalie 0.2–0.4 goals.
  • Early season ratings lean on last season. They sharpen after 3–4 football games or 8–10 basketball/hockey games.
  • The market is sharp. This backtest measures accuracy against final scores, not against betting lines. Treat small edges (under ~3%) as noise and track results in Track record before trusting real money to it.
